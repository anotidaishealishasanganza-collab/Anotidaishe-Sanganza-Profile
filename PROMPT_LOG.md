## Prompt I used

```
Context: I'm a Software Engineering student at Africa University putting
together a personal portfolio page for a coursework assignment. It's my
first graded piece of work this semester.

Goal: Draft a short "About" section bio to go near the top of the page.

Constraints: Keep it to 3-4 sentences, first person, confident but not
overly formal, and mention that I'm still early in learning web
development. Avoid generic phrases like "I am passionate about
technology."

Format: Plain paragraph text with no headings, ready to paste into an
HTML <p> element.
```

## Reflection

The AI's draft was already close to what I wanted, so I mainly tightened the
wording in the second and third sentences and made the ending more specific to
this page rather than a generic sign-off. It reads like something I'd actually
say about myself once I toned down a couple of the more formal-sounding phrases.
